Why this exists

A carrier lookup is easy. A defensible operating decision is not.

Fraud, stale records, ambiguous names, different insurance requirements, low-exposure safety data, partial API failures, and local policy all meet at the same desk. The pack makes those seams visible.

  • Find the right carrierSearch by identity, geography, fleet, insurance, equipment, cargo, or lane without calling candidates approved.
  • Verify the operating factsReconcile authority, insurance, contacts, equipment, safety evidence, and related entities before a load or onboarding decision.
  • Apply your policyUse SearchCarriers qualification reports or a complete customer-owned rule set. Missing evidence never silently passes.
  • Operate at panel scaleResume bulk runs, surface stale rows, preserve successful work, format review queues, and propose TMS changes with rollback evidence.
  • Monitor honestlySeparate a configured watch from an actual event feed, then route supplied changes into bounded review work.

One path from search to operations.

Use only the stages the decision needs. Each stage can stop on ambiguity, stale evidence, access limits, or missing policy.

  1. 01

    Source

    Find carriers by identity, geography, lane, cargo, equipment, fleet, insurance, or authority filters.

  2. 02

    Resolve

    Confirm the legal entity, docket, USDOT, VIN, contacts, and relationships before evaluating it.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Read authority, filed insurance, safety, inspection, crash, equipment, and identity-risk evidence.

  4. 04

    Qualify

    Apply a named SearchCarriers qualification or a complete customer-owned policy with reproducible results.

  5. 05

    Operate

    Build review queues, dashboards, reports, exports, watches, and dry-run TMS change sets.

  6. 06

    Retain

    Keep source, as-of time, missing evidence, policy owner, next action, and run receipts together.

The trust boundary

Evidence in. Named policy around it. Human authority at the end.

SearchCarriers supplies research evidence. Grok, Claude, or another MCP client can orchestrate the same tools, while the skills preserve the evidence and prevent a model, missing field, or search result from becoming official truth.

The tools can

  • Resolve identifiers and compare records
  • Expose missing, stale, or partial evidence
  • Apply an explicit policy or upstream qualification
  • Produce review work, reports, exports, and dry runs

The tools do not

  • Create an official FMCSA safety rating
  • Guarantee a carrier, person, load, or transaction
  • Invent customer thresholds or approve a carrier by default
  • Support employment, credit, housing, insurance, or other FCRA-regulated eligibility decisions
