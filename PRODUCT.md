# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript served by Caddy. The repository owns the catalog surface; existing demo directories remain independent deployment artifacts.

## Users

Prospective customers, partners, collaborators, and builders who need to inspect real Intent Solutions work before deciding what to explore or discuss.

## Product Purpose

Intent Demos is the public proof catalog for the Intent Solutions network. It helps a visitor find a relevant working system, inspect the live artifact or source, and understand what evidence exists behind it.

## Positioning

The catalog pairs runnable work with current, attributable proof: public source, external adoption, merged upstream contributions, and dated measurements.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Intent Solutions, Learn, Labs, Evals, OMA, Tons of Skills, and JeremyLongshore.com. Most browse without an account and may be evaluating the team from a phone during a conversation or event.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all existing public demo routes and the `/api/forms/contact` contract.
- Keep Labs, Evals, Learn, OMA, and Demos distinct while explaining how they connect.
- Use only verifiable public metrics and date every time-sensitive claim.
- Attribute Jeremy Longshore's upstream pull requests to him; do not imply client, employer, or partner relationships from a merged contribution.
- Do not present retired n8n experiments as flagship work.

## Brand Commitments

Intent Solutions is founder-led and team-built. Demos must feel like a sibling of Learn and OMA: direct language, visible operating evidence, restrained orange, strong black-and-white structure, and no vendor-dependent identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Eighteen existing runnable routes under `/home/jeremy/demos`.
- Public GitHub repositories and repository metrics queried through the GitHub API.
- Skills.sh installation data at `https://skills.sh/b/jeremylongshore/tons-of-skills-marketplace`.
- Live estate properties at `*.intentsolutions.io`, `tonsofskills.com`, `diagnosticpro.io`, `hustlestats.io`, and `scorecardecho.com`.

## Product Principles

- Lead with inspected proof, not a capability inventory.
- Separate team work, founder contributions, and third-party adoption accurately.
- Make every primary card open something real.
- Keep the network legible without collapsing its distinct properties.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The catalog must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic landmarks, responsive reflow, and WCAG AA contrast.
